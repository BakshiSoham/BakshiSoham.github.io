I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Statistics at the University of Michigan, advised by Professor Snigdha Panigrahi. I develop inference-aware adaptive algorithms: data-driven procedures that choose variables, trees, winners or policies through an explicitly randomized rule, so that the question asked about the selected object can still be answered with valid uncertainty quantification. My work spans causal effect moderation in mobile health, decision trees, inference on winners, and the reliability of models trained on noisy or self-generated data. More on the research page and in my research notes.

I was born in Kolkata, Bengal and grew up in the hostel of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyapith, Purulia. Before joining UofM, I completed my Bachelor's in Mathematics (Hons) from Indian Statistical Institute in Bangalore, India.

If you're interested in discussions, research collaborations, or potential connections, don't hesitate to contact me at bakso@umich.edu. My name is Soham Bakshi, but everybody calls me Bakshi!
