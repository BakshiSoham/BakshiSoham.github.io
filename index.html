I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics in the Department of Statistics at the University of Michigan. Broadly, my research focuses on developing innovative statistical methodologies that enable meaningful inferential insights in today's data-driven science. I am advised by Professor Snigdha Panigrahi and my current works are aimed at leveraging randomization to facilitate flexible and tractable inference for data-adaptive methods.

I was born in Kolkata, Bengal and grew up in the hostel of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyapith, Purulia. Before joining UofM, I completed my Bachelor's in Mathematics (Hons) from Indian Statistical Institute in Bangalore, India.

If you're interested in discussions, research collaborations, or potential connections, don't hesitate to contact me at bakso@umich.edu. My name is Soham Bakshi, but everybody calls me Bakshi!
